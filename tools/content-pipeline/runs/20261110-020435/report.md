### Round 1: sent back

Critic (revise): The draft passes the structural checks: 24 weapons, an 8/8/8 split with 4 Green Valley and 4 Forest in each category, one humble Hallow Wood Sword starter at the bottom of the band, and every damage and speed value inside its band. Every ability and passive is allowed for its weapon type, and the tone is sincere with no banned words. Five things need revising. (1) Many weapons in the same category are reskins: four Ranged weapons share the exact same ability pool, the two crossbows share both pools, three Magic weapons share one arcane pool, and the fire and frost pairs are near-copies. (2) Damage is not matched to speed: whichever way attackSpeed is read, one whole class of weapon is clearly better than the other. (3) Every pool has exactly 4 entries, so every Exalted copy rolls the same fixed kit. (4) Frost weapons in the Forest rely on Mountain-coded Frost Shards. (5) Several drop sources don't make sense, such as wolves, boars and forest predators dropping crafted bows and slings, and an iron-scrap golem dropping a stone mace.

### Round 3: sent back

Critic (revise): This is a solid draft on the hard rules. It has 24 weapons, 8 per type, split 4 and 4 between the two regions. Every damage curve rises and stays inside its band. Every ability and passive is allowed for its weapon type. The starter is right: one-handed, at the bottom of the band, and it keeps the WoodenSword id, so the starter link isn't broken. Damage per second is consistent: about 21 at Exalted for Green Valley and about 30 for Forest, with the starter lowest at 16. The tone is sincere throughout, with no banned words and no "you".

It needs a revision for these reasons:
- **Ranged weapons repeat each other.** 5 of the 8 have exactly the same ability pool, and the three two-handed Forest Pierce weapons are near-copies. Winter's Mark is never used.
- **Magic pools are copied across regions.** Briarbind Wand matches Dewglass Rod, and Hollowlight Lantern matches Goblin Shaman Rattle. Both of those also clash with their weapon's idea, and Oblivion is never used.
- **One drop source is wrong for the item.** Golemstone Mace drops from a bone guardian.
- **Two weapons point at placeholder loot tables that always give Exalted.** Meadow Shortbow and Hollowlight Lantern would become guaranteed Exalted drops from Grey Wolf (the opening region's 20 HP wolf) and from Bonewrought Depths (a 2-room dungeon).
